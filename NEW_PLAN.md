Controls:
    s - shuffle (if playing a collection)
    p - play/pause
    d - download (if not already)
    l - like (dislike if already liked)

streamline song Cold - Give
    Takes in "Cold - Give"
    Plays the song "Cold - Give"

streamline playlist Rock Music
    Takes in "Rock Music"
    Plays the playlist "Rock Music"

streamline search "Imagine Dragons"
    Takes in "Imagine Dragons"
    Searches for songs by "Imagine Dragons"
